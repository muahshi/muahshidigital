🛑 1. Yeh Business Kya Hai? (Ekdum Saral Bhasha Mein)

🌾 Khet aur Threshing Machine ka Udaharan:

Jaise gaon mein jab gehu ki fasal kat-ti hai, toh kisaan apna gehu saaf karne ke liye Threshing Machine (Kup) wale ko bulata hai aur usko ghante ke hisab se paisa/bhaada deta hai.

Theek usi tarah, aaj ke zamane mein badi-badi computer companies ko bade video banana, photo saaf karna, aur dimaag wale computer (AI/ChatGPT) chalane ke liye bohot badi machine chahiye hoti hai jise GPU Server kehte hain.

Humara Kaam: Hum Indore mein aisi hi do high-power power-house computer machines laga rahe hain. Duniya bhar ke log internet ke zariye humari machine par apna kaam bhejenge, humari machine unka kaam karke degi, aur badle mein humein Dollar ($) mein bhaada (Rent) milega!
